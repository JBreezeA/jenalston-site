Quick answer

What are GEO and AEO services?

GEO (generative engine optimization) and AEO (answer engine optimization) services help a brand appear in AI-generated answers. The work combines prompt testing, answer-first content, entity and citation building, and measurement, so AI tools mention, cite and link to your brand.

Customers increasingly ask AI tools for recommendations instead of scrolling through search results. Generative engine optimization is the practice of earning a place in those answers, and answer engine optimization applies the same thinking to direct-answer features like Google's AI Overviews.

This work builds on SEO rather than replacing it. Google's own guidance on AI features and your website confirms that strong SEO foundations still matter for AI Overviews and AI Mode. My AI search optimization services start from those foundations, then focus on the signals AI tools rely on.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank your pages in search results. GEO aims to get your brand mentioned, cited or recommended inside AI-generated answers. GEO builds on SEO, because AI tools often draw on sites that already rank well. My beginner's guide to GEO explains the difference.

How do you measure results in AI search?

With structured prompt testing tracked over time: mention rate, citation rate, link inclusion, share of voice and prompt visibility. See how I measure AI share of voice.

How long does it take to see results?

Some changes can show up in AI answers within days of publication, as they did in my enterprise work. Authority built through digital PR and mentions takes longer, usually months. Measure monthly to see the trend.

Do you work on projects, retainers or contracts?

All three: one-time projects such as an audit and roadmap, monthly retainers, or contract and fractional roles inside your team.
