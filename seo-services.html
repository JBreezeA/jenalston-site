What do freelance SEO services include?

Freelance SEO services usually cover technical SEO, keyword and competitor research, content and on-page optimization, link building and reporting. Working with one experienced consultant means one strategy and one point of contact, without agency overhead.

Every engagement starts with what your business needs from search: leads, sales or visibility. The work follows the fundamentals in Google's SEO Starter Guide, applied with the judgment that comes from years of real campaigns.

[3] Technical SEO

Technical SEO services

Technical SEO makes sure search engines and AI crawlers can find, read and understand your site. As a freelance technical SEO consultant, I can audit, fix and document:

  • Crawling and indexing issues, including robots.txt, sitemaps and canonical tags
  • Site speed and mobile experience
  • Structured data and schema markup (JSON-LD)
  • Internal linking and site architecture
  • Redirects and site migrations

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What's included in technical SEO services?

A crawl and indexing review, site speed and mobile checks, structured data, internal linking and redirects, delivered as a prioritized fix list. I can implement the fixes or work with your developer.

Do you offer one-time projects or ongoing SEO?

Both. Many clients start with an audit and roadmap, then move to a monthly retainer or a contract role for ongoing work.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I support agencies with overflow and specialist work, including technical SEO, content, digital PR and GEO.

How does SEO connect to AI search?

AI tools like Google's AI Overviews draw heavily on well-optimized sites, and Google's guidance on AI features and your website says SEO best practices still apply. Strong SEO is the foundation for GEO and AEO.
