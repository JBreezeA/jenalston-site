[02] Adobe Firefly · via Envision · 2026

Directory listings built for AI search

The challenge

Earn trusted placements for Firefly's AI generator tools in the places search engines and AI assistants both read, without the spam risk of old-school directory blasts.

The approach

I screened directories by authority, traffic and AI-visibility score, used a backlink-gap audit to find directories that listed competitors but not Firefly, and wrote unique listings that pair the brand with each target keyword. Submissions were staggered, and open comment sections became a second source of links.

[08] Harkins (concept) · Responsive web

Express snack pick-up, with accessibility up front

The website companion to the snack app, built for moviegoers who'd rather order ahead than miss the opening scene. As the UX designer leading the site, I went from a site map and paper wireframes to a high-fidelity responsive prototype. Usability testing reshaped the navigation: confusing arrows became a floating nav menu, accessibility controls moved into the top bar, and payment became quick pop-up steps.

[09] ConCerns 4 Safety · Responsive web & app

A safe, anonymous way for students to speak up

A responsive site and app that lets K–12 students anonymously report concerns about a peer's safety, straight to school and district staff, with peer-to-peer chat and resources built in. As lead UX designer and researcher, I designed the full flow, from location and school to the concern form and confirmation, keeping navigation simple and calm for teens. Testing moved logout, navigation and the urgent-help message to where students could find them quickly.
