Generative Engine Optimization helps your brand appear in AI-written answers, not just search results. If customers ask ChatGPT or Gemini about your category, it matters. Start with my beginner's guide to GEO.

Structured prompt testing, tracked over time: mention rate, citation rate, share of voice, link inclusion and prompt visibility. Here's my full method.
