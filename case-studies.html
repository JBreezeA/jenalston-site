[01] Contracted with Adobe (via Envision) · 2022 – Now

Enterprise GEO & AEO program

The challenge

A brand as big as Adobe already gets named in AI answers. The harder job was getting Adobe Express's specific product pages cited and linked for non-branded, customer-style questions in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and proving it with data.

The approach

I owned keyword mapping and competitor gap analysis for Adobe Express, matching target pages to the searches and AI prompts customers use and finding where competitors were getting cited instead. Then I mapped AI answers for 6 product lines to the questions customers ask (mostly non-branded) and tested which content changed them across 23 third-party articles, each built around 5 to 10 customer-style prompts. I designed the prompt-testing framework and prompt libraries, then tracked every placement by hand across AI Overviews, Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, scoring brand mentions, target-page links, article citations and share of voice. I also built the AI visibility workbook that other Adobe teams adopted, and managed 5+ vendors and 4+ freelance writers.

From publishing to AI Overview and ChatGPT inclusion for priority campaigns

Results by product

  • Hoodie maker: cited at position 1 in an AI Overview for a non-branded prompt, with the fabulousmomlife.com article ranking #1 on Google. On a second placement (neworldsmagazine.com), ChatGPT went from no visibility at the first check to naming Adobe Express, with a link to the hoodie maker, within five weeks (on a follow-up prompt).
  • Business card maker: cited at positions 3 and 4 in AI Overviews, #1 on Google, and linked by ChatGPT (tesseract.academy). A second placement got the product page linked in an AI Overview (accountingbyte.com).
  • AI logo maker: placement articles ranked #1 on Google (buddymagazine.org, amourvert.com), the product page was linked at position 1 in an AI Overview, and ChatGPT linked it for 2 of 3 tested prompts.
  • AI flyer generator: showed up at positions 2 and 5 in AI Overviews with a link to the article (adaptnetwork.com), and the product page was linked in an AI Overview for a second placement (coruzant.com).
  • What didn't land: two placements got no ChatGPT mentions at all, and the mood board articles ranked #1 on Google but never appeared in AI answers before that product moved to Firefly. That's also the proof that AI visibility wasn't automatic, even for Adobe.

[02] Contracted with Adobe (via Envision) · Firefly · 2026

Directory listings built for AI search

The challenge

Earn trusted placements for Firefly's AI generator tools in the places search engines and AI assistants both read, without the spam risk of old-school directory blasts.

The approach

I screened directories by authority, traffic and AI-visibility score, used a backlink-gap audit to find directories that listed competitors but not Firefly, and wrote unique listings that pair the brand with each target keyword. Submissions were staggered, and open comment sections became a second source of links.
