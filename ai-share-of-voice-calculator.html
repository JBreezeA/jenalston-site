New to this? How to get and copy an AI answer Start here
  1. Open the AI tool in a private window (Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome, Cmd+Shift+N on a Mac) so your past chats don't affect the answer. For ChatGPT, go to chatgpt.com. For Google AI Overviews, go to google.com and type your question into the regular search box. The AI Overview is the summary at the top of the results. If Google opens a chat-style AI Mode page instead, that's a different tool.
  2. Start a new chat and ask one question, the way a customer would. Leave your brand name out. For example: “Who are the best bakeries for wedding cakes in Denver?” or “What's the best project management tool for small teams?”
  3. Copy the whole answer. In ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Perplexity, click the Copy icon (two overlapping squares) under the answer. In Gemini, it may be inside the ⋮ menu. For a Google AI Overview, click and drag from the first word to the last, then press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac). Don't use Ctrl+A, which selects the whole page.
  4. Pick the tool you used under “Where did this answer come from?”, then paste the answer into the box with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  5. Include the sources. Citations are counted from the links in the answer, so copy the source list too. In Gemini, click ⋮ (More) under the answer, choose Sources, copy the list and paste it right under the answer. When you paste, the calculator also pulls real source links out of the copied page and adds them under the answer for you. One exception: if a Google AI Overview only gives you coded links (they start with /goto?url=), use the steps below. If your copy didn't include the sources, you can add them:
    1. Go back to the answer and find its sources. Depending on the tool, they show up as a Sources button, small numbered links, link cards beside the answer (Google AI Overviews), or a list at the bottom.
    2. Click Sources if there's a button, so the list opens.
    3. Right-click a source link and choose Copy link address (on a Mac trackpad, click with two fingers).
    4. Click at the end of your answer in the box, press Enter, and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac). Each link goes on its own line.
    5. Repeat for each source.
    No time? Brand mentions still count if you added names in step 1. You just won't get a page-level or domain-level share.
  6. Want a steadier number? Ask the same question again in a fresh chat, click “+ Add another answer” under the box, and paste the new answer.

[3] How it works

The formula behind the number

AI share of voice = (your brand's mentions ÷ total mentions of all tracked brands) × 100

Citation share of voice = (citations of your website ÷ citations of all tracked websites) × 100

Page citation share = (answers citing your page ÷ answers citing all tracked pages) × 100

It's the same core formula Semrush uses for AI share of voice.

“All tracked brands” means you plus the competitors you entered. Mentions show who AI tools name. Citations show who they send people to: a citation is any time a brand's website appears in an answer, as a source or a link, including any page on it. Track specific pages, like a product or service page, to see exactly which of your pages AI tools rely on. Those visits are worth winning: traffic from generative AI sources to U.S. retail sites rose 1,200% between July 2024 and February 2025, according to Adobe Analytics.

The calculator counts each brand, site and page once per answer, no matter how often it's named or linked, so one long or repetitive answer can't inflate the total. It's the standard for AI answers. Web addresses in source lists count as citations, not mentions.

Look at the per-platform numbers, not just the blended one. A healthy overall share can hide a platform where you're invisible, and that platform is usually where the next round of work should go.

A few rules for clean numbers:

  • Leave out branded prompts. If a prompt includes your brand name, the AI will almost always mention you.
  • Run each prompt two or three times. AI answers vary from run to run, so more answers give a steadier number.
  • Keep conditions consistent. Use a fresh chat, the same prompts and the same competitors each month.

[4] Quick questions

Quick questions

What is AI share of voice?

AI share of voice is the percentage of brand mentions in AI-generated answers that belong to your brand, compared with the competitors you track. It shows how often ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews name you when people ask about your category.

How does the calculator count mentions?

Each brand counts once per answer, so a single long or repetitive answer can't inflate a brand's share. It matches whole words and ignores capitalization. Web addresses, like yourbrand.com in a source list, count toward your citations instead of your mentions.

Is my data saved or shared?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you paste is sent anywhere. Your entries carry over when you switch between quick start and advanced. Your last session is saved only in this browser so you can pick up where you left off, and Clear all removes it.

How many prompts should I test?

One is enough to start. Paste a single answer and you'll see your share of voice right away. For a steadier number, ask the same question two or three times, in a fresh chat each time, since AI answers vary from run to run. Add a few more questions your customers would ask when you're ready, and keep the same questions and competitors each month so your numbers stay comparable.

What's the difference between mentions and citations?

A mention is when an AI answer names a brand. A citation is when it includes the brand's website as a source or link. Mention share of voice shows who AI tools talk about, and citation share of voice shows who they send people to. Mentions build awareness, while citations are what bring visitors to your site.

Can I track citations for a specific page?

Yes. Enter a page URL, like https://jenalston.com/ai-visibility-audit, in step 1, and the calculator shows your page-level share next to your domain-level share. Pages are matched exactly, so citations of other pages on your site aren't counted toward them.
