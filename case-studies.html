[02] Adobe Firefly · via Envision · 2026

Directory listings built for AI search

The challenge

Earn trusted placements for Firefly's AI generator tools in the places search engines and AI assistants both read, without the spam risk of old-school directory blasts.

The approach

I screened directories by authority, traffic and AI-visibility score, used a backlink-gap audit to find directories that listed competitors but not Firefly, and wrote unique listings that pair the brand with each target keyword. Submissions were staggered, and open comment sections became a second source of links.
