Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a big budget for SEO?

No. Many of the highest-impact fixes, like consistent listings, clear service pages and reviews, cost more time than money. We start with what will make the biggest difference.

Can I do some of it myself?

Yes. My beginner's guide to GEO has a checklist you can start this week. I can handle the parts that need specialist skills.

Do you work with businesses outside your area?

Yes. I work remotely with small businesses and startups across the US.

How long does SEO take for a small business?

Fixes like listings and FAQ content can show results within weeks. Rankings and reputation built through reviews and mentions usually take a few months.
