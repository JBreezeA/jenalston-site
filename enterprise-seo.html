Quick answer

What is enterprise SEO?

Enterprise SEO is search optimization for large organizations with big sites, many stakeholders and formal approval processes. It focuses on strategy, governance, repeatable frameworks and reporting, so many teams can improve search and AI visibility in the same direction.

At enterprise scale, the hard part is rarely knowing what to fix. It's getting the right work prioritized, approved and shipped across teams, then proving it worked. Large sites also bring technical challenges of their own, which Google covers in its guidance on managing crawl budget for large sites.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you work alongside our existing agencies and vendors?

Yes. Much of my enterprise work has involved coordinating vendors and freelance writers to a shared standard and calendar.

How do you report results to leadership?

Through dashboards built around the metrics leadership cares about, including AI share of voice, citation rate and prompt visibility, alongside traditional SEO and lead metrics.

Do you work as a contractor inside larger teams?

Yes. I work on contract and fractional engagements as an embedded part of your team, as well as on defined projects.

Does GEO replace our SEO program?

No. GEO builds on SEO. Google's guidance on AI features and your website says SEO best practices still apply, so the strongest programs run both together.
